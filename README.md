A cozy spot-the-difference game set in a lantern-lit study. Two nearly identical scenes sit side by side — warm lamplight, bookshelves, and quiet corners — and 7 subtle differences hide between them. Spot them all to win: tap a difference to mark it, use a hint when you are stuck, and reach the win screen once every difference is found.

## How to play

Open `game/index.html` in a browser and compare the two scenes. Click (or tap) each difference you spot — found differences stay marked, and a hint button points you toward one you missed. Find all 7 differences to reach the win screen.
